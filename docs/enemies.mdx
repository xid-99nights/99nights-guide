# Entities

Entities are a fundamental part of the game. Players encounter various entities and animals, some hostile, passive, or neutral.

## Missing Children
These are the children you must rescue (optional for multipliers).

- Dino Kid
- Kraken Kid
- Squid Kid
- Koala Kid

## Passive Animals
Friendly to players.

- Bunny
- Horse
- Kiwi Bird
- Turkey

## Traders (NPCs)
- **Pelt Trader**: Trades Bunny Foot, Wolf Pelt, Alpha Wolf Pelt, Bear Pelt for items. Rewards increase.
- **Furniture Trader**: Trades Mossy Coins for Furniture (decoration).
- **Fairy**: Trades flowers for Seed Packs (price scales, max 80).
- **Bird Watcher**: Trades Feathers for Fuel Canisters and Gem of the Forest Fragment. (No longer in game)
